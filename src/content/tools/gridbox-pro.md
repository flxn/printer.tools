## Gridbox Pro: Gridfinity Bins, Baseplates and Shadowboxes From a Photo

Gridbox Pro is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that designs Gridfinity bins, baseplates sized to your drawer, and shadowboxes traced from a photo of your tools. Try it free with no time limit and design as much as you like, then $150 once unlocks exporting STL and 3MF.

### Highlights
- Photograph a tool next to the printed scaler tile, and it traces at its real size
- Check the fit with digital calipers before you print
- Baseplates fill your drawer edge to edge and split into pieces your printer fits
- Bins and shadowboxes bigger than your print bed split into pieces that join with pins and magnets
- Share a .gridboxpro file, and anyone can open it in the free app and change every setting for their own drawer

### Best Results Tips
- Hold the phone as high as you can, zoom in at least 2x and keep the tool in the middle
- Print the ShadowFit test first; it takes a fifth of the time of the full shadowbox or less
