## Coaster2STL

Make a matching set for the coffee table, a coaster for your desk, or a small gift with your own artwork. Coaster2STL lets you design the coaster in your browser and download the STL files to print it.

Start with one of seven shapes, from a simple circle to a scalloped edge, then set the size, thickness, and rim. Add an icon, upload an SVG or image, or draw a motif with mirror or radial symmetry. Curved lettering follows the edge, while background patterns leave room around your artwork and text.

The design options extend to the underside. Add a recess for cork or pockets for rubber feet, and choose raised, engraved, or flush inlaid details on top. Export one STL or separate parts for multi-color printing. A mug in the preview helps you judge the coaster's size before printing.

For a starting point, try "Surprise me" for a themed design with matching artwork and lettering. The community gallery lets you share a finished coaster or open someone else's design and make it your own.

Coaster2STL is free and open source. Model generation and STL export happen in your browser. Publishing to the gallery uploads the design and makes it public.
