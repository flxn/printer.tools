## Businesscard2STL

Businesscard2STL creates 3D-printable business cards in your browser. Add your name, job title, company, and contact details, then choose a layout and preview the result before exporting an STL file.

Choose from six layouts: Classic, Split, Accent bar, Centered, Minimal, and Monogram. Customize fonts, card dimensions, and colors, add contact icons, or upload an SVG logo. The QR code can contain a website link, a vCard, or custom text.

Details can be raised, engraved, or inlaid. Export a single STL, or enable separate parts to download a ZIP of STL files for multi-color printing. Binary and ASCII STL exports are supported, along with PNG previews.

The tool is free and open source. Model generation and file export run on your device, so your contact details and designs stay in your browser.
