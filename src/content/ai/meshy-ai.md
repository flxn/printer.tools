## Meshy - AI Pipeline for 3D Assets

**Meshy** is a browser-based studio that compresses the entire 3D asset pipeline into a few clicks. Type a prompt, upload a concept image, or remix an existing mesh and Meshy will generate geometry, PBR textures, rigging, and even motion clips. Teams can collaborate in shared workspaces or automate tasks via the Meshy REST API.

### Key Features
- **Text-to-3D and image-to-3D** generation modes with style presets for realistic, stylized, voxel, and low-poly art directions
- **Smart remesh controls** to dial triangle counts, convert to quads, and match poly budgets before exporting to Unreal, Unity, Blender, or printers
- **Texture and animation tooling** including AI prompt editing, bulk texture batches, and an integrated library of 500+ game-ready motions
- **Scale-friendly pipeline** that queues 50+ jobs simultaneously and supports multilingual prompting plus enterprise governance controls

### Print & Production Notes
Meshy’s exports cover FBX, GLB, OBJ, STL, 3MF, and USDZ, so you can go straight from concept to review or physical prototype. For 3D printing, use the smart remesh slider to generate a mid-poly STL, inspect thin walls, and optionally bake textures for multi-material prints.
