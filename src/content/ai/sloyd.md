## Sloyd - Hybrid AI & Parametric Generator

**Sloyd** blends AI text generation with a curated library of handcrafted parametric templates, so you can produce game-ready props, characters, and mechanical parts without traditional modeling skills. Start with a prompt, apply textures, then fine-tune the geometry using intuitive sliders and toggles.

### Why Creators Like It
- **Multiple creation modes**: Text-to-3D labs, image-to-3D conversion, and a template editor with hundreds of adjustable assets
- **Instant customization**: Adjust bevels, proportions, and design motifs interactively, or let the AI suggest variations on the fly
- **Clean exports**: Download low- or high-poly meshes with UVs ready for Blender, Unreal, Godot, or 3D printing workflows
- **Flexible pricing**: A free tier plus credit packs and studio plans, with enterprise options for custom-trained styles

### Best Practices
When prepping for 3D printing, use the template editor’s dimensional controls to thicken walls and simplify ornamentation. After exporting an STL, run a quick manifold check—Sloyd’s meshes are generally print-friendly, but it’s smart to spot thin overhangs before slicing.
